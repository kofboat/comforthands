Text Message Opt-In

Comfort Hands Staff Notifications

Comfort Hands Home Healthcare, LLC sends work-related text messages only to staff who agree to them. This page shows exactly how staff opt in.

How staff opt in

Staff opt in on our online employment application at comforthands.net/apply, in the “Consent and Authorizations” section. They must choose Yes to the statement below. The Yes/No choice is not pre-selected, and no one is texted unless they choose Yes.

I agree to receive text messages (SMS) from Comfort Hands Home Healthcare, LLC at the mobile number on this application. The messages are about my work: shift and clock-in/clock-out reminders, scheduling updates, and reminders about my employment compliance requirements and due dates (such as annual health requirements and certification renewals). Messages never include patient information. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.
