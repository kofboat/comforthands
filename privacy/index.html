Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Comfort Hands Home Healthcare, LLC (“Comfort Hands,” “we,” “us”) is a home health agency in New Jersey. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website and online employment application, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Text messaging. Mobile information and text-message consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

1. Information we collect

When you apply for employment or use our staff pages, we collect what you give us:

  • Contact details: name, address, phone number, email address.
  • Application details: date of birth, Social Security number, work history, education, licenses and certifications, references, driver’s license information, and documents you upload such as a résumé or identification.
  • Authorizations and signatures: your consent choices, including the choice to receive text messages, and your electronic signatures.
  • Technical data: your browser and device type and spam-protection signals (reCAPTCHA) used to keep the form safe from automated abuse.

This website is not for patient health information. Patient information is handled separately under our HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

2. How we use it

  • To review your application and decide on employment, including checking references and, with your authorization, running a background check.
  • To onboard and manage staff: employment paperwork, scheduling, compliance records, and payroll-related records.
  • To contact you, including by email and, only if you agreed, by text message about your work.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations, and to protect the security of our systems.

3. Text messages

If you agree to text messages, we use your mobile number only to send work-related messages such as shift reminders and compliance reminders. We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or your text-message consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Our messaging provider processes messages only to deliver them for us. Reply STOP at any time to stop messages.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run our business under confidentiality obligations (for example, Google for secure document storage and email, and Twilio for text delivery).
  • A background-screening company, if you authorize a background check.
  • Government agencies, insurers, or others when required by law or needed to comply with licensing and program requirements.

5. How we protect and keep it

Application records are stored in access-restricted systems, and information is encrypted while it travels between your browser and our systems. We keep application and employment records for as long as needed for hiring, employment, and legal record-keeping requirements, then securely delete them.

6. Your choices

  • You may ask to see, correct, or delete your information, subject to record-keeping laws, by contacting us below.
  • You may stop text messages at any time by replying STOP, or by telling the office.

7. Children

Our website is for adults seeking employment. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
